Organising team & partners

A small volunteer committee planned and executed the day, from registration to the final prize presentation.

Convener & Chief Education Officer

Pastor Olatunde “Moore” Ayo-Moore

Founded the challenge and led day-to-day communication with all twenty participating schools through registration and the run-up to the event.

Executive Director, Ivory Educational Resources

Bukunmi Olatunde

Oversaw registration and scorekeeping on the day, and has since announced plans for a Maths and Science Challenge later this year.

Head of Program & Logistics

Babatunde Awoyemi

Founder & CEO, Techbase Consultant Services. Authored the event execution plan and coordinated the full programme rundown from 7:30 AM check-in through prize presentation.

Head of Media

Powered by Techbase Consultant Services

Techbase Consultant Services is an Ibadan-based STEM and technology education consultancy, working with schools and individuals across Nigeria on coding and robotics training, curriculum and teaching aids, web and app development, and practical AI tooling for classrooms.

Techbase’s Founder & CEO, Babatunde Awoyemi, served as Head of Program & Logistics for the Akinyele Spelling Challenge 2026, running the full event rundown from contestant check-in to prize presentation.
